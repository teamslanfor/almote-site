FAQs

Questions from buyers

Who do you supply?

Supermarkets, retail stores, commercial buyers, institutions and government agencies in Puerto Rico.

Are you registered as a federal supplier?

Yes. We are registered in SAM.gov (UEI TBF2WJMVM4G5, CAGE 9YA47) with primary NAICS 424490, and hold SBA WOSB and HUBZone certifications.

Which products do you carry?

Cookies and biscuits, chocolates and confectionery, canned goods and seafood, mineral water, sports and isotonic drinks, sodas and grocery products. See our Products page.

Can you source products we don’t see listed?

Ask us. Importing and sourcing is part of what we do, so send us your requirement and we’ll check availability.

How do I request a quote?

Use our quote form, call (787) 383-2424 or message us on WhatsApp.

Need a reliable food supplier?

Tell us what you need – products, volumes and delivery location – and we’ll prepare a quote.
