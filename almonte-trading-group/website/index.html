Who we are

Reliable food distribution & wholesale supply from Puerto Rico

Almonte Trading Group L.L.C. is a Caguas-based food importing, representation and distribution company. We supply quality food products, groceries, confectionery, beverages and packaged goods.

We serve supermarkets, commercial buyers, institutional customers and government agencies with a focus on reliable sourcing, product availability and professional distribution.

  • Local roots: founded in Caguas in 2022 and supplying local supermarkets.
  • Federal supplier: four federal contracts reported, worth over $2.5 million.
  • Long-term focus: dependable supply and responsive service.
