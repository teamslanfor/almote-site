Our story

Building dependable food supply for Puerto Rico

Almonte Trading Group L.L.C. was established on July 18, 2022 in Caguas, Puerto Rico. We import, represent and distribute food and beverage products, and we supply local supermarkets with cookies, chocolates, tuna, confectionery, mineral water, isotonic drinks and sodas.

In 2024 we set out to grow beyond local distribution. With support from the Puerto Rico Federal Contracting Center we registered in SAM.gov, created our Dynamic Small Business Search profile and pursued SBA certifications. In less than a year we won four federal contracts worth over $2.5 million, tripled our workforce and expanded our product and service lines.
